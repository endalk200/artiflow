This page adds `remark-math` and `rehype-katex` to the Fumadocs MDX pipeline. The source stays compact while the output remains crisp and accessible.

## Inline notation

Einstein's mass–energy relation, $E = mc^2$, sits naturally inside a sentence. So does Euler's identity, $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

## Display equations

The Pythagorean theorem can stand on its own:

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$

A more involved expression is just as direct:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

## A small derivation

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Start with a geometric series

    For $|r| < 1$:

    $$
    S = 1 + r + r^2 + r^3 + \cdots
    $$
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Multiply by the ratio

    $$
    rS = r + r^2 + r^3 + \cdots
    $$
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Subtract and solve

    $$
    S - rS = 1 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad S = \frac{1}{1-r}
    $$
  </Step>
</Steps>
